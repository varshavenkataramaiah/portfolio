// about

Developer, researcher, teacher.

I'm a full-stack developer working with the MERN stack (MongoDB, Express, React, Node.js), based in Bengaluru. I build web applications end to end from data models and REST APIs to responsive, component-driven interfaces with a bias toward shipping things that actually run.

I hold an MSc in Embedded Systems & IoT from Newcastle University (Merit) and a BTech in Electronics & Computer Engineering from REVA University (9.05 CGPA). My background sits where hardware meets software, which shapes how I write code: an eye for performance, real constraints, and systems that hold up in production. Alongside eight filed Indian patents and an IEEE ICAECC 2023 publication, I've increasingly focused on building software tools and web experiences because a good idea deserves a good interface.

I served as an Assistant Professor of Computer Science & Engineering at Cambridge Institute of Technology North Campus, a tenure during which kept me close to fundamentals and constantly explaining the "why" behind the build.
